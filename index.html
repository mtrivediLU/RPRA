Job description

Compliance Data and Business Intelligence Analyst.

RPRA is Ontario's circular economy regulator. The role supports compliance reporting, data stewardship, resource recovery analytics, Blue Box and other RRCEA programs, stakeholder engagement, and evidence based decision making.

Core mission

Support waste reduction and resource recovery by enforcing compliance with Ontario producer responsibility laws, providing registry services, and reporting publicly on progress toward a circular economy.

Key duties

  • Develop and maintain compliance reporting procedures and policies.
  • Provide data literacy guidance and training for compliance staff.
  • Produce operational summaries, risk assessment reports, presentations, trends, risks, and recommendations.
  • Develop and monitor KPIs and corporate performance measures.
  • Support inspector workflows, registry improvements, public reporting, budgeting, fee setting, and process enhancements.

Data stewardship

  • Define compliance data definitions and rules.
  • Maintain standardized reporting logic.
  • Establish data quality controls and exception handling.
  • Maintain documentation and audit trails for repeatable reporting.
  • Support controlled access and appropriate handling of sensitive or confidential information.

Qualifications and tools

  • 4+ years in BI, analytics, reporting, or program analytics.
  • Excel, Salesforce CRM data, data visualizations, quantitative and qualitative analysis.
  • Policy analysis, research methodology, regulatory and compliance frameworks.
  • Project coordination, prioritization, multitasking, written and verbal communication.
